Which regions appear and how they behave come from the CAPY_SHELL_* settings (see conf.py). Below Tailwind's `md` breakpoint the left-nav sidebar becomes an off-canvas drawer opened by a burger button in the header; at `md` and up it's a docked column. The drawer opens and closes on its own (a checkbox + CSS, so it works without JavaScript); static/capy_shell/drawer.js adds the keyboard and focus behavior on top.
